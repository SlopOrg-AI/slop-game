## Rules / Data
- **Engagement-scoped initiative (D5.24):** initiative is scoped per engagement, not per scene. Multiple simultaneous engagements within one scene (e.g., a 1v1 street fight and a fire rescue down the block) each run their own separate initiative track, resolving simultaneously. Any condition bleed from an engagement into the shared scene applies at end of round — see `14-scenes-conditions.md`. Distinct from Open #5 in `00-steer.md` (initiative model for multiple actors *within* one engagement — still open).
