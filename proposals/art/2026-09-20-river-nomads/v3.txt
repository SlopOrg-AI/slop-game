A River Nomads river settlement, built as flat cut paper laid in layers: long narrow wooden boats in the front layer, a stilt-built plank dock street behind them, a market row of hanging nets and woven cargo bundles behind that, and flat bands of mist, water and distant hills behind everything. The dock runs straight across the picture from edge to edge, seen side on from across the water, every layer parallel to the picture plane and sitting slightly in front of the one behind it with a small soft shadow between them, no gradient shading inside any shape. The people are flat paper standees read as silhouettes first, dark simple shapes with almost no interior detail, each carrying a single saturated accent colour in an otherwise muted indigo and ochre scene. Salvaged pre-collapse things are lashed into ordinary river-life materials, a small battered cassette deck and a coiled wire antenna tied to a boat's prow, coloured signal lanterns strung on cords between poles, in the quiet travelling mood of a Hiroshige woodblock print.
