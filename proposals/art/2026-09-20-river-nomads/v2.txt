A River Nomads river settlement cut out of coloured paper: every boat, stilt house, net and person is a separate flat paper shape with a clean white cut edge, laid in overlapping layers that each drop a small hard shadow onto the sheet behind, with no gradient shading anywhere and no texture in the colour. The settlement runs left to right straight across the picture in flat parallel layers seen from across the water, long narrow wooden boats in front, a plank dock street on stilts behind them, a market row of hanging nets and woven cargo bundles behind that, with no vanishing point and no deep perspective. Salvaged pre-collapse machinery is lashed into ordinary river-life materials, a battered tape player and a coiled wire antenna strapped to a boat's prow, coloured signal lanterns strung on cords between poles. Behind it all the river, the mist and the distant hills are flat bands of colour like a woodblock print, muted indigo and ochre throughout, with one saturated accent colour on each figure.
