A River Nomads settlement built as a flat cut-paper illustration: long narrow wooden boats and stilt-built dwellings strung in one line along a wide river, plank docks standing in for streets, a river market of hanging nets and woven cargo bundles. Every boat, building and figure is made of flat layered paper shapes with clean cut edges and solid unshaded colour, read silhouette first, like paper dolls standing on a paper set. Salvaged pre-collapse machinery is lashed into ordinary river-life materials, a cassette deck and a coiled wire antenna strapped to a boat's prow, coloured signal lanterns strung on cords between poles. Behind the settlement the water recedes into layered woodblock-print mist and distant hills in flat bands of colour, in a muted indigo and ochre palette with one saturated accent colour on each figure, in the travelling mood of a Hiroshige Tokaido print.
