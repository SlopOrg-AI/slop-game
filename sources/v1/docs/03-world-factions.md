# 03 — World, cultures and factions

Imported 2026-09-20 from Shinobi Master chat text. Culture categories below were requested by the user; specific faction names, borders, histories, species and visual assets have not been recovered or established by this import.

## Established direction

An anachronistic post-post-apocalyptic world with cassette cyber-funk elements, alien and surprising ecology, forgotten architecture, reused ruins, and strong faction/character aesthetics. Latest visual correction emphasizes Ghibli/Nausicaä and Cowboy Bebop; see `02-art-direction.md`.

The requested world board includes jungle, swamp, desert, pilgrims, nomads, city dwellers, villagers and river people. These overlap as environments and social identities; they are not necessarily eight exclusive political factions. The user wants cultures on a world map with individual profiles, flora/fauna and grounded material culture.

The broader campaign includes competing factions and NPC agendas shaped by personality, culture and religion. The current development MVP remains one human-playtested duel; world-board scope does not expand the engine MVP.

All details in this table are assistant proposals, not finalized lore. Real-world inspirations in the source were reference ingredients, not one-to-one faction identities.

## Proposed worldbuilding method

Build from ecology → livelihood → materials → architecture → clothing → tools → combat silhouette. A region profile can connect environment, settlement pattern and political tension. Give alien life an ecological role and a distinctive biological rule, then explore human uses, hazards and cultural meaning.

Distinguish the old builders' intended architecture from subsequent damage and present reuse. Post-post-apocalypse can mean adapted societies with trade, rituals and everyday life rather than constant collapse. These are useful coordinator proposals pending selection.
