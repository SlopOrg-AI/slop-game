# 02 — Art direction

Imported 2026-09-20 from **Vision Board Lore Bible** and **Assemble faction image board**. This is a synthesis of accessible chat text, not a recovery of their attached documents or images. See `07-shinobi-master-context.md` for provenance and precedence.

## Established user direction

- Handcrafted paper cut-out characters and tabletop-like terrain. Minimalist shapes, flat contrasting colors, saturated rather than desaturated.
- Paper Mario primarily means theater, puppets and staged pieces. NITRO GEN OMEGA primarily means staged camera cuts and an affordable route to animation. Neither reference locks a production pipeline.
- The user rejected boards as too Naruto-like and requested stronger Ghibli, especially Nausicaä, and Cowboy Bebop influences. Naruto remains useful for readable action, not as the default world/costume template.
- Priorities recorded in the board chat: flat faction colors, animation-ready clothing, silhouette readability. Use flowing/posable shapes without clutter.
- Alien flora and fauna should coexist with grounded material culture. Environments should explain how people live and what they wear and build.
- River Nomads should carry hints of western aesthetics.
- Earlier screen feedback favors noir, late Victorian/industrial and frontier/caballero influences; remove Japanese-character/calligraphy motifs. Cassette cyber-funk should not overwhelm the interface.

## Combat UI feedback to carry into future exploration

- No tactical grid; preserve cinematic perspective, depth and spacing.
- Minimal drawers: prefer buttons, icons, thin strips and small inspection tooltips.
- Explore a color-coded radial/fan ability selector centered on the player character.
- Insight inspection may use direct targeting, as in the cited Zero Company/XCOM interaction references, without adding a grid.
- The user requested an initiative strip with action-capacity information and a player icon that moves as actions are queued or removed. This is historical visual intent, not a finalized rules change: current local mechanics separate initiative/reach from trick-taking resolution order and have no fixed action count. Reconcile these before implementing a new UI.

## Proposed translation, not additional canon

- Ghibli/Nausicaä: ecological scale, handmade technology, practical garments, strange life and settlements adapted to their environment.
- Cowboy Bebop: asymmetry, practical workwear, worn technology, industrial frontiers and cinematic negative space.
- River Nomads: short dusters, broad hats, neckerchiefs, river satchels, ferry poles, ropework, patched canvas, boats and reclaimed dock infrastructure.
- Three to five saturated colors per culture is an assistant-proposed working palette rule. The numerical 45/30/15/10 influence split in the board chat was also a proposal, not an approved requirement.
- Avoid obvious Hidden Leaf rooflines, standard shinobi vests and headband shorthand as a practical interpretation of the user's request to reduce Naruto influence.

## Next art work

Continue from the revised River Nomad direction when requested. Evaluate silhouettes, clothing movement, poses and terrain together. Mark new concepts exploratory until selected. Generated reference images remain in the original chats and were not available through text retrieval; do not claim to have inspected or recreated them.
